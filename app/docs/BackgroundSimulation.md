# Background Simulation

The Background Simulation encompasses the entirety of the Living Grid's autonomous operations.

Core Principles:
- "Everything lives": Organizations, markets, and NPCs operate continuously.
- "The player is NOT the center of the world": Events happen regardless of player intervention.
- "Incremental execution": Simulation logic is distributed across ticks to maintain performance.

State is persisted via the `SaveManager`, ensuring continuity across sessions.
