# Corporate Organizations Specification

## Overview

The **Corporate Grid** in AbyssOS 0.6.0 introduces 105 global corporate entities spanning 11 industry sectors. Each organization represents an autonomous actor with its own domain, IP subnet, data center facilities, server nodes, security clearance tiers, budget, and employee base.

## Industry Sectors

1. **Technology & Software** (`TECHNOLOGY`): AI engineering, software development, cloud systems.
2. **Finance & Banking** (`FINANCE`): Investment banks, asset management, cryptocurrency vaults.
3. **Healthcare & Biotech** (`HEALTHCARE`): Pharmaceuticals, genomic research, medical equipment.
4. **Government Agency** (`GOVERNMENT`): Civil administration, regulatory oversight, municipal networks.
5. **Defense & Intelligence** (`MILITARY`): Strategic defense contractors, avionics, intelligence networks.
6. **Academic Research** (`UNIVERSITY`): Universities, high-energy physics labs, supercomputing centers.
7. **Telecom & ISP** (`ISP`): Backbone network carriers, fiber infrastructure, satellite grids.
8. **Cloud Infrastructure** (`CLOUD_PROVIDER`): Public cloud datacenters, edge computing meshes.
9. **R&D Enterprise** (`RESEARCH_LAB`): Quantum computing labs, synthetic biology institutes.
10. **Global Media Conglomerate** (`MEDIA`): Broadcasting, streaming media, content distribution networks.
11. **Industrial Manufacturing** (`MANUFACTURING`): Heavy industry, automotive, robotics assembly.

## Organization Attributes

- **ID & Code**: Unique identifier (e.g. `org-AEG101`, `org-APX102`).
- **Domain**: Registered corporate domain (e.g. `aegis-corp.com`, `apex-financial.net`).
- **Subnet**: Dedicated CIDR block (e.g. `10.40.0.0/16`).
- **Security Tier**: 1 (Low) to 5 (Top Secret / Military Grade).
- **Reputation & Budget**: Corporate credit standing and annual capital pool.
- **Data Centers**: 1 to 3 facilities with location tracking (e.g. New York, Frankfurt, Tokyo).
- **Server Nodes**: 15 to 48 servers per data center totaling 2500+ global corporate server nodes.
