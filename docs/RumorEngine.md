# Underground Rumor Engine (v1.4.0)

## Overview
The `RumorEngine` drives dynamic information propagation, leaks, and underground events across the Dark Layer in AbyssOS.

## Core Capabilities
- **Rumor Generation**: Dynamically creates rumors regarding corporate vulnerabilities, raid alerts, zero-day discoveries, and market shifts.
- **Credibility System**: Assigns credibility scores (0.0 - 1.0) to rumors. High-credibility rumors often precede actual living world incidents.
- **Underground Events**: Spawns urgent event notifications (`UndergroundEvent`) such as global node raids, corporate leaks, or zero-day drops.
- **Forum Thread Injection**: Automatically converts high-impact rumors into discussion threads on cypherpunk forums.
