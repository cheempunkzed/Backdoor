# Underground Factions System (v1.4.0)

## Overview
Milestone 1.4.0 introduces distinct underground factions that shape the Dark Layer politics, hidden forums, and corporate conflict.

## Factions

### 1. Cypher Syndicate (`[CYPHER]`)
- **Ideology**: Radical information freedom, zero-day research, anti-corpo leaks.
- **Hidden Services**: `blackvault.onion`, `cyphers-forum.onion`
- **Minimum Trust**: 100

### 2. Void Sect (`[VOID]`)
- **Ideology**: Anarcho-cypherpunk, grid disruption, black market trade, chaos engineering.
- **Hidden Services**: `darkmarket.onion`, `void-nexus.onion`
- **Minimum Trust**: 300

### 3. Aegis Red (`[AEGIS]`)
- **Ideology**: Defensive counter-intelligence, mercenary bounty hunting, grid stability.
- **Hidden Services**: `aegis-intel.onion`
- **Minimum Trust**: 200

### 4. Ghost Network (`[GHOST]`)
- **Ideology**: Elitism, stealth relays, high-level corporate espionage.
- **Hidden Services**: `zero-day.onion`
- **Minimum Trust**: 500

## Trust Tiers
- **UNTRUSTED** (< 50 rep): Access restricted.
- **NEUTRAL** (50-200 rep): Standard member access.
- **ACCEPTED** (200-500 rep): Access to faction private hidden services and threads.
- **INNER_CIRCLE** (> 500 rep): Elite access, exclusive market listings, and mission contracts.
